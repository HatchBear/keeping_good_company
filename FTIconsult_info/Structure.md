# **Client-Facing Generalist Consulting Roles**

Across many FTI practices, a commonly cited core ladder is Consultant → Senior Consultant → Director → Senior Director → Managing Director → Senior Managing Director. 
### **Consultant**

- Client-facing: **Yes**
- Geography: **Global** (common across practices; hiring patterns vary by country)
- Business unit: **Practice-specific** (e.g., Corporate Finance, Forensics, Technology, Strategic Communications)
- Typical degree required: **Bachelor’s degree**
- Typical work experience at entry: **0–3 years** (campus hire or early-career)
- Description: **Entry-level delivery contributor** supporting analyses, building client-ready outputs, and working directly with engagement teams on high-stakes matters.
- Typical duration in this role: **2–3 years**
- Typical next role(s): **Senior Consultant**

### **Senior Consultant**

- Client-facing: **Yes**
- Geography: **Global**
- Business unit: **Practice-specific**
- Typical degree required: **Bachelor’s or master’s** (MBA helpful in some tracks)
- Typical work experience at entry: **3–8 years**
- Description: **Senior workstream owner** who leads defined modules, mentors junior staff, and manages day-to-day client interactions for a portion of the engagement.
- Typical duration in this role: **2–4 years**
- Typical next role(s): **Director**

### **Director**

- Client-facing: **Yes**
- Geography: **Global** (practice and market dependent)
- Business unit: **Practice-specific**
- Typical degree required: **Not standardized** (credentials and experience matter most)
- Typical work experience at entry: **8–15+ years**
- Description: **Engagement leader** responsible for supervising client engagements, providing technical expertise, and guiding Senior Consultants and Consultants on delivery. 
- Typical duration in this role: **2–5 years** (varies)
- Typical next role(s): **Senior Director**

### **Senior Director**

- Client-facing: **Yes**
- Geography: **Global**
- Business unit: **Practice-specific**
- Typical degree required: **Not standardized**
- Typical work experience at entry: **12–20+ years**
- Description: **Senior delivery and growth leader** who oversees larger portfolios, shapes complex proposals, and manages senior client stakeholders while mentoring Directors and Managers-of-workstreams.
- Typical duration in this role: **Indefinite** (performance-based senior band)
- Typical next role(s): **Managing Director**

### **Managing Director**

- Client-facing: **Yes**
- Geography: **Global**
- Business unit: **Practice leadership**
- Typical degree required: **Not standardized**
- Typical work experience at entry: **15–25+ years**
- Description: **Senior relationship owner** responsible for originating work, leading major engagements, and shaping practice priorities and talent across a segment or specialty.
- Typical duration in this role: **Indefinite**
- Typical next role(s): **Senior Managing Director**

### **Senior Managing Director**

- Client-facing: **Yes**
- Geography: **Global**
- Business unit: **Top practice leadership** (often global or regional leadership roles)
- Typical degree required: **Not standardized**
- Typical work experience at entry: **18–30+ years**
- Description: **Top-tier practice leader** who owns major client portfolios and often leads a global practice area (e.g., trial/arbitration support) while driving growth and standards firmwide. 
- Typical duration in this role: **Indefinite**
- Typical next role(s): **Global leadership**, board/advisory careers

### **Graduate Consultant**

- Client-facing: **Yes**
- Geography: **Region-specific** (more common in some non-U.S. programs)
- Business unit: **Practice-specific** (often Economic & Financial Consulting or similar)
- Typical degree required: **Bachelor’s or master’s**
- Typical work experience at entry: **0 years** (graduate entry)
- Description: **Graduate entry point** into consulting teams, typically aligned to an analyst/consultant-equivalent band depending on local structure and practice needs.
- Typical duration in this role: **1–2 years**
- Typical next role(s): **Consultant**, **Senior Consultant** (local ladder dependent)

## **Client-Facing Specialist Roles**

### **Director, Restructuring**

### **Economic Consultant**

- Client-facing: **Yes**

## **Data / Analytics / AI / Product Roles**

FTI’s Technology segment includes dedicated data engineering and analytics delivery, with workflows and platforms commonly used in e-discovery and investigations (e.g., Relativity, Reveal/Brainspace, Nuix). 

### **Data Engineer**

- Client-facing: **Sometimes** (often delivery-heavy and client-IT coordinated)
- Geography: **Global**
- Business unit: **Technology** (data engineering)
- Typical degree required: **Bachelor’s** in CS/engineering/IT or related
- Typical work experience at entry: **3–5+ years** for many mid-level postings
- Description: **Data foundation builder** who designs pipelines, ETL, and defensible workflows to manage and process very large client datasets for disputes, investigations, and discovery matters. 
- Typical duration in this role: **2–4 years**
- Typical next role(s): **Senior Data Engineer**, **Director** (technical leadership track)

### **Data Scientist**

- Client-facing: **Yes** (often embedded with matter teams)
- Geography: **Global** (hiring hubs vary)
- Business unit: **Technology** (analytics and AI)
- Typical degree required: **Master’s** (common) in CS/statistics/applied math; PhD for some roles
- Typical work experience at entry: **2–8+ years** (level-dependent)
- Description: **Analytics and ML practitioner** who prototypes and implements AI/ML solutions on evolving client datasets and translates results into decision-relevant outputs for investigations and discovery work. 
- Typical duration in this role: **2–4 years**
- Typical next role(s): **Lead Data Scientist**, **Director** (analytics leadership)

### **Lead Data Scientist**

- Client-facing: **Yes**
- Geography: **Region-specific** (varies by hiring location)
- Business unit: **Corporate Finance & Restructuring** (Data and Technology Transformation) or **Technology** (role-dependent)
- Typical degree required: **Master’s or PhD** (common) or equivalent experience
- Typical work experience at entry: **6–12+ years**
- Description: **Analytics workstream leader** who owns end-to-end AI/ML development (feature engineering through validation), leads project workstreams, and ensures solutions align with business objectives. 
- Typical duration in this role: **Indefinite** (senior band)
- Typical next role(s): **Managing Director** (digital leadership path), practice leadership roles

### **Director, Review Manager**

- Client-facing: **Yes** (delivery leadership on matters)
- Geography: **Region-specific** (varies by market)
- Business unit: **Technology** (e-discovery operations)
- Typical degree required: **Bachelor’s**
- Typical work experience at entry: **8–15+ years** in e-discovery delivery or legal tech operations
- Description: **Delivery leader** who runs review workflows and technology-enabled discovery operations, often using platforms such as Relativity, Brainspace/Reveal, and Nuix-based tooling. 
- Typical duration in this role: **Indefinite**
- Typical next role(s): **Senior Director**, **Managing Director**

## **External Advisor Titles**

### **Senior Advisor**

- Client-facing: **Yes** (selectively, typically senior-client moments)

- Typical duration in this role: **Affiliation- or role-based**

- Typical duration in this role: **Time-bounded**
